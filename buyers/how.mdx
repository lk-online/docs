## Account

- Register on the site’s **[registration](https://ship-around.com/register/)** page. Buyer approval is described as **almost instant** in the [FAQ](https://ship-around.com/faq/).
- You may be asked for documents later for due diligence; personal email sign-ups may require proof of ID.
- **No membership fee** for buyers; you pay the **order total** shown, with **no hidden buyer fees** on product purchases per the FAQ.

## Shop and categories

The **shop** groups products into **top-level categories** (product families) and **subcategories**. Use navigation and **search** to move from broad areas (for example **Machinery & Fluid**) down to the exact product type you need. See [Categories and coverage](/platform/categories-and-coverage) for how the tree is organized.

## Simple vs variable products

- A **simple** product is one buyable line: one price, one stock signal, one set of core specs.
- A **variable** product is a **parent** page that groups **variations**—separate purchasable rows (different sizes, grades, lengths, and other SKU-defining combinations). You always choose **one variation** to buy, not an abstract “parent.”

Understanding that split helps when you **compare** several lines or read **lead time** per variation.

## Compare and Watchlist

Use **Compare** to put listings side by side on specifications that matter for your decision. Use **Watchlist** to save items while you check budget, class rules, or internal approvals. Both live on the main website alongside the shop.

## Inquiries

Where the product or workflow supports it, **Inquiries** offer a **quote-style** path instead of immediate checkout—useful when you need formal offers or clarifications before committing.

## Vendors directory

The public **Vendors** page lists participating suppliers. You can use it to understand who stands behind a storefront and to explore their published assortment.
