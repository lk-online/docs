## Who arranges shipment

- **Products in Ship-Around warehouses** fulfilled by Ship-Around may use **Ship-Around-arranged transport** as described in the [FAQ](https://ship-around.com/faq/).
- **All other products** — The **vendor** is responsible for shipping **unless** the listing clearly states otherwise.

Always read the **listing** and checkout text for the specific line you buy; transport is not identical across every product class.

## Who pays freight

- **Buyers usually pay transport** unless the vendor **chooses** to cover freight and **states that on the listing**.
- If you are unsure, clarify before confirming the order—especially for heavy or project cargo.

## Lead time

**Lead time** on the **variation** you select reflects stock, production, and what the vendor published. If the field is vague or missing, treat it as a signal to confirm via **Inquiry** or FAQ contact paths before relying on a tight project schedule.

## Tracking

**Order tracking** was **not offered** at the time of the FAQ wording on the main site; that may change. Watch **announcements** on [ship-around.com](https://ship-around.com/) or ask via the **Contact** and FAQ channels if you need a current statement for a live order.
