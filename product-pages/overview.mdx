Ship-Around splits public **product discovery** into two main page types:

1. **Leaf category pages** (product archives)—a filtered grid of every listing attached to the **deepest** category in the shop tree.
2. **Single product pages**—the full detail page for one **parent** product (simple or variable), including variations, documents, and vendor actions.

Both sit alongside the main [Shop](https://ship-around.com/shop/) entry. Mega-menu links jump straight to **`/categories/...`** leaves; the **last child** of each branch is the URL that actually lists products in a grid.

## URL patterns (live site)

| Page type | URL pattern | Real examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leaf category | `/categories/{parent}/{child}/` | [Pneumatic Fenders](https://ship-around.com/categories/fenders/pneumatic-fenders/) · [PP Ropes](https://ship-around.com/categories/mooring-lines/pp-ropes/) |
| Single product | `/products/{family}/{subcategory}/{slug}/` | [Trelleborg Pneumatic Fender](https://ship-around.com/products/fenders/pneumatic-fenders/trelleborg-pneumatic-fender/) · [PP Rope (Preneur Marine)](https://ship-around.com/products/mooring-lines/pp-ropes/pp-rope-preneur-marine/) |

Other routes you will see from these pages include **brand** links (`/brand/{slug}/`), **stock location** links (`/stock-location/...`), and **vendor** storefront links (`/vendor/{slug}/`).
