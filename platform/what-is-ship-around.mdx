---
title: "What is Ship-Around?"
description: "A B2B platform connecting buyers and suppliers for marine, offshore, and related industrial equipment."
---

**Ship-Around** is a business-to-business platform where procurement teams can discover, compare, and purchase equipment and supplies from approved suppliers. Suppliers list products in structured categories so buyers can search, filter, and evaluate options in one place.

## Who it is for

- **Buyers** — Companies and teams that source marine, offshore, oil and gas, and related industrial products.
- **Vendors (suppliers)** — Manufacturers, distributors, and service providers who sell compliant products through the platform.

## What you can do on the platform

- Product discovery through the **shop** and category navigation.
- Tools such as **Compare** and **Watchlist** (available on the main site) to shortlist and track items.
- **Inquiries** for quote-style workflows where that applies.
- **Vendor storefronts** — A public directory of participating suppliers.

## What stays between buyer and vendor

Fulfillment details (who ships, from where, and on what terms) follow the rules stated on each listing and in the site’s [FAQ](https://ship-around.com/faq/). Ship-Around sets platform rules for payments, returns, and disputes as described there.
