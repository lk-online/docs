## Who can sell

Vendors offer products related to **marine, offshore, and oil and gas** industries. Listings must comply with applicable laws and platform rules. Illegal, unsafe, or restricted products are not allowed (see [Terms](https://ship-around.com/terms/) and FAQ).

## Joining and fees

- Register via the site’s [registration](https://ship-around.com/register/) page and choose the vendor account type.
- An **on-boarding** contact will follow up to complete setup (FAQ).
- **No membership fee** to become a vendor.

## Your responsibilities

- You are **fully responsible for the accuracy** of your listings: prices, stock, lead time, and descriptions.
- You may sell **new**, **used**, and **lease** offerings where allowed (FAQ).
- Assign a **store manager** as the main point of contact for operational communication with Ship-Around (FAQ).

## Fulfillment and transport

- For products **not** in Ship-Around’s fulfillment network, **you** arrange shipment unless you state otherwise on the listing.
- **Buyers usually pay freight** unless you choose to pay and say so clearly on the listing.

## Payments and payouts

Checkout payments go **through Ship-Around**. Payouts are described in the FAQ (e.g. **bank transfer**). Discounts and coupons can be configured as explained there.

## Returns, refunds, and disputes

Ship-Around’s **returns and refunds** process may involve both parties. Disputes are handled with both sides in mind (FAQ).
