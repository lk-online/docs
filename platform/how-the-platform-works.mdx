This page summarizes the typical flow. For binding details, use the [FAQ](https://ship-around.com/faq/), [Terms](https://ship-around.com/terms/), and [Privacy Policy](https://ship-around.com/privacy-policy/) on the main site.

## 1. Discover and compare

Buyers browse the **shop**, use search and filters, open product pages, and can use **Compare** and **Watchlist** on the website to evaluate options.

## 2. Order or inquire

Depending on the product and listing, the buyer completes checkout or uses **Inquiries** where that path is offered. The buyer must follow any proof-of-payment or timing rules described in the FAQ.

## 3. Payment clearing

To protect buyers and sellers, **payments are made to Ship-Around**, not directly to the vendor in normal checkout. Funds are cleared according to the process described in the FAQ before a sale is treated as finalized.

## 4. Fulfillment and logistics

- **Products fulfilled from Ship-Around warehouses** — Ship-Around may arrange transportation as described in the FAQ.
- **All other products** — The **vendor** is responsible for transportation unless the listing clearly states otherwise. **Buyers normally pay transport costs** unless the vendor chooses to cover them and states that on the listing.

Delivery timing depends on payment clearance, the shipping method chosen at checkout, and **lead time** on the listing.

## 5. After the sale

Returns, refunds, and disputes are handled through **Ship-Around’s internal process**, which may involve both parties. See the FAQ sections for buyers and vendors.

## Communication and support

Buyers and vendors can use the **contact** options listed on [Contact us](https://ship-around.com/contact-us/) and the [FAQ](https://ship-around.com/faq/) (email and regional details).
