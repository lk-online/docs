The **shop** on [ship-around.com](https://ship-around.com/shop/) groups equipment and supplies into **top-level categories** (product families). Each family contains more specific subcategories so buyers can narrow down to the exact type of item they need.

## Top-level shop categories

As shown on the shop page, the main areas include:

- **Deck, Mooring & Fenders**
- **Cargo Handling**
- **Machinery & Fluid**
- **Electrical & Automation**
- **Safety & Life-Saving**
- **Security**
- **HVAC & Thermal**
- **Accommodation**
- **Chemicals & Consumables**
- **Tools & Workshop**

Within each area you will find further splits (for example fender types, anchor types, valve families, and so on). The live site always shows the **current** full tree.

## Vendors and assortment

The public **Vendors** page lists participating supplier names. Assortment grows as vendors publish listings; not every subcategory may have live products at all times.

## Why this structure matters

Consistent categories support **search**, **filters**, and **comparison** so buyers can match technical requirements (size, rating, material, and similar fields) without hunting through unrelated products.
