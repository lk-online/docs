## Account and approval

- Register on the site’s [registration](https://ship-around.com/register/) page. Buyer approval is described as **almost instant** in the [FAQ](https://ship-around.com/faq/).
- You may be asked for documents later for due diligence. Personal email sign-ups may require proof of ID.

## Fees

- **No membership fee** for buyers.
- You pay the **order total** as shown; the FAQ states **no hidden buyer fees** on product purchases.

## Finding and evaluating products

- Browse the **shop** and category pages.
- Use **Compare** and **Watchlist** on the main site to compare items and save them for later.
- Use **Inquiries** where available for quote-style needs.

## Payment

- Currently **direct bank transfer** is offered; more methods may be added over time (see FAQ).

## Shipping and delivery

- **Ship-Around-arranged transport** applies only to products **in Ship-Around warehouses** and fulfilled by Ship-Around.
- Otherwise the **vendor** ships; **you normally pay transport** unless the listing says the vendor covers it.
- **Order tracking** is not offered at the time of the FAQ; that may change—watch announcements on the site.

## Returns, refunds, and disputes

Ship-Around runs an **internal returns process** and **usually handles refunds**. Disputes are escalated with the goal of a fair outcome for both sides. See the buyer section of the [FAQ](https://ship-around.com/faq/) for specifics.

## Cancelling an order

Cancel as soon as possible if you change your mind. Repeated cancellations without good reason may affect your account (see FAQ).

## Support

- Email addresses listed in the FAQ for general and technical topics.
- Regional phone numbers on [Contact us](https://ship-around.com/contact-us/).

## If you also list products as a vendor

Dashboard and listing guides are in the **Vendors** tab (for example [Why your listing quality matters](/vendors/why)).
